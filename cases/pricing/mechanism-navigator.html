Pricing Mechanism Navigator

A guided learning map for reading pricing cases by mechanism. Start with the pricing decision you are trying to understand, then choose the case that best exposes the decision tension behind the bill.

01 / Start Here

Start With Your Pricing Question

Use the question that feels closest to the decision in front of you. The point is not to classify the case perfectly; it is to find the pricing mechanism that makes the tradeoff visible.

Commitment Am I trading discount for commitment? Fulfillment Is the bill driven by operational effort? Visibility Is fee visibility changing the buyer reaction? Usage Does usage cross an allowance boundary? Platform Does activity flow create a second price layer? Organization Does team scale change willingness to pay? Trigger path Does a product purchase lead into subscription economics?
02 / Mechanism Clusters

Mechanism Cluster Cards

Each cluster names the mechanism, the decision tension, and the first cases to study. Read one anchor case first, then compare a neighboring cluster when the pricing logic starts to overlap.

01

Commitment and Flexibility

Commitment pricing Utilization risk Forecastable demand Flexibility premium

Decision tension: lower effective rates versus the risk of being wrong about future demand.

AWS Reserved Instances / Savings Plans pricing

Study this when the mechanism trades lower effective rates for commitment. The case helps readers see how utilization risk moves onto the buyer when forecast confidence replaces flexibility.

02

Operational Burden and Fulfillment Complexity

Cost-to-serve Fulfillment effort Capacity burden Service boundary

Decision tension: operational convenience versus the visible cost of complexity.

Amazon FBA fulfillment and storage pricing

Study this when pricing reflects handling, storage, size, and capacity burden. The case makes seller convenience versus margin surprise concrete for cost-to-serve pricing.

IKEA Netherlands delivery fulfillment pricing

Study this when product price and fulfillment service price are separated. The case shows low product-price perception colliding with visible delivery effort at the service boundary.

03

Fee Incidence and Price Visibility

Buyer-visible fees Seller-side fees Fairness perception Conversion pressure

Decision tension: checkout clarity versus where the economic burden appears to land.

Airbnb service-fee incidence pricing

Study this when the same economic fee can be shown to different sides of a marketplace. The case helps explain buyer-visible checkout friction versus seller-side payout deduction.

04

Usage Allowance and Boundary Crossing

Value metric Allowance Rollover Overage threshold

Decision tension: predictable subscription comfort versus threshold-triggered bill changes.

HP Instant Ink page-count subscription pricing

Study this when pricing uses an allowance, rollover, and overage boundary. The case helps readers see how a value metric can feel predictable until usage crosses a threshold.

05

Platform Activity Economics

Subscription layer Transaction economics Value capture Activity flow

Decision tension: fixed plan clarity versus monetizing the activity flowing through the platform.

Shopify transaction platform pricing

Study this when subscription access is only one layer of platform monetization. The case helps explain transaction-linked value capture as commerce activity grows.

06

Seat Growth and Organizational Complexity

Seat-based pricing Tier ladder Team scale Operational complexity

Decision tension: linear user growth versus deeper governance, integration, and workflow needs.

monday.com seat-based work management pricing

Study this when the bill scales with users and capability tiers. The case helps connect team growth to B2B seat-and-tier pricing rather than treating seats as a simple headcount meter.

07

Hardware-to-Subscription Trigger Path

Hardware trigger Membership dependency Recurring capture Post-purchase monetization

Decision tension: upfront product ownership versus ongoing access to the connected experience.

Peloton hardware-to-membership pricing

Study this when a hardware purchase creates the path into recurring monetization. The case helps readers see how post-purchase value capture depends on continued membership value.

03 / Study Method

How To Use This Navigator

Step 1 Start with the decision question.

Choose the question that best matches the pricing problem, even if the company or industry is different.

Step 2 Read the anchor case for its mechanism.

Focus on what changes the bill, who carries the risk, and where the buyer crosses a pricing boundary.

Step 3 Compare a neighboring mechanism.

Move sideways when the logic overlaps, such as usage thresholds versus capacity burden or commitment versus flexibility.

Step 4 Return to the full library.

Use the complete pricing case archive when you want to browse all cases by company, industry, and review date.
